## Aims and scope

*Computational Statistics & Data Analysis* is an international journal devoted to
methodological research and applications in computational statistics and data
analysis. It is organised into four refereed sections:

1. **Computational Statistics** — the impact of computers on statistical
   methodology: Bayesian computing, bioinformatics, data mining, machine
   learning, neural networks, numerical and optimisation methods, parallel
   computing, and the development, evaluation, and validation of statistical
   software and algorithms.
2. **Statistical Methodology for Data Analysis** — biostatistics, chemometrics,
   classification, density estimation, experimental design, image analysis,
   psychometrics, and robust procedures; methods include bootstrapping, Bayesian
   modelling, functional data analysis, mixture models, model selection,
   time-series, and longitudinal/multilevel analysis.
3. **Special Applications** — work at the interface of statistics and computing,
   simulation experiments, and applied analyses.
4. **Annals of Statistical Data Science** — data-driven research with substantial
   computational and statistical methodological content, with an emphasis on
   reproducibility.

**Scope constraint:** papers must include either a computational or a
data-analysis component. Purely theoretical papers are returned to authors.
Simulation studies are expected to be thorough across parameter settings, avoid
over-generalisation, and document limitations. Computational results should
describe the random-number generators, algorithms, hardware, languages, and
software used.

Software and algorithms may be submitted alongside a manuscript, and the journal
publishes research papers, short communications, review papers, and book
reviews.

## Review process and turnaround

- Submissions are handled through Elsevier's Editorial Manager system.
- ScienceDirect reports **4 days to first decision** (median, including desk
  decisions), **106 days to a decision after review**, **278 days to acceptance**,
  and **6 days from acceptance to online publication**.
- Elsevier does not publish an official acceptance rate; third-party estimates
  range from roughly 8% to 13%.
- The explicit requirement for a computational or data-analysis component means
  papers are screened for scope fit early; purely theoretical submissions are
  returned without review.

## Open access and costs

- Hybrid journal: publication via the subscription route is free; open access
  costs **USD 3,540** (excluding taxes).
- An optional graphical abstract is encouraged.
- Elsevier's research-data and reproducibility guidelines apply; a data
  availability statement and, where relevant, code/software should accompany the
  submission.

## Formatting and submission

- Use Elsevier's `elsarticle` LaTeX class (or the Word template). Numbered
  sections (1, 1.1, 1.1.1, …); the abstract is unnumbered; acknowledgements
  precede the references; appendices are lettered A, B, with equations numbered
  (A.1) and so on.
- References may follow any consistent style at submission; the journal's style
  is applied at proof stage. DOIs are encouraged, data references are tagged
  `[dataset]`, and preprints must be identified as such.
- Artwork: EPS for vector figures; TIFF at ≥300 dpi for halftones, ≥1000 dpi for
  line art, and ≥500 dpi for combination figures; use Arial, Courier, Times, or
  Symbol fonts.
- Submit through Editorial Manager; include a cover letter and suggested
  reviewers where requested.

## Fit for the residual-plot paper

The manuscript *Automated Assessment of Residual Plots with Computer Vision
Models* satisfies the journal's key scope rule: it has both a computational
component (a trained computer vision model, image encoding, calibration, and an
implementation in R/Python via `autovi`) and a data-analysis component (residual
diagnostics, lineup-based visual inference, and comparisons with conventional
tests and human judgments). It would sit naturally in **Computational Statistics**
or **Statistical Methodology for Data Analysis**, and the reproducibility
emphasis of the **Annals of Statistical Data Science** section is also relevant
given the released code and data.

Points to weigh:

- Explicit, welcoming scope for computer vision and machine learning applied to
  statistical methodology.
- Monthly publication and a larger annual volume than *Statistics and Computing*.
- No APC on the subscription route — a practical advantage if open access is not
  required.
- Longer median time to acceptance (~9 months), and a comparatively low
  acceptance rate.
- Simulation studies must be documented thoroughly (RNGs, algorithms, hardware,
  software) to meet the journal's methodological standards.
