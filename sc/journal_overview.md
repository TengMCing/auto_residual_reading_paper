## Aims and scope

*Statistics and Computing* publishes work at "the interface between the
statistical and computing sciences." It covers two directions:

- **Statistical ideas applied in computing** — machine learning, computer
  vision, data analytics, information retrieval, database technology, and the
  statistics of software errors.
- **Computational approaches to modelling, prediction, and analysis** — methods
  for analytically intractable problems, including bootstrap resampling, Markov
  chain Monte Carlo, sequential Monte Carlo, approximate Bayesian computation,
  search and optimisation, stochastic simulation, graphical models, image
  processing, tomography, inverse problems, and uncertainty quantification.

It also covers statistical graphics and computing environments, computer
algebra, and large/big-data analytics. The journal is refereed and publishes
original research, review papers, discussion papers, special issues, and a
book/software review section.

## Review process and turnaround

- Submissions go through Springer's Editorial Manager system.
- Springer reports a **median submission-to-first-decision time of 3 days**.
  This is dominated by desk decisions and should not be read as the length of a
  full review.
- Author-reported experience is typically **6–12 weeks** for a full review
  cycle (third-party data, not an official statistic).
- Acceptance rate is not published by Springer.

## Open access and costs

- The journal is currently **hybrid**: subscription-route publication is free;
  optional open access carries an APC.
- From **1 January 2027** the journal becomes **fully open access**. Submissions
  received from **1 July 2026** are subject to an APC if accepted and published,
  unless a waiver or institutional agreement applies.
- APC: **€2,390 / $2,990 / £2,090**, plus VAT or local taxes.
- Springer Nature waivers and institutional/consortium agreements may cover the
  charge; eligibility should be checked at submission.
- In July 2026, Retraction Watch reported that **18 associate editors resigned**,
  effective 31 December 2026, in protest at the mandatory APC. This may affect
  editorial capacity during the transition, so it is worth watching the journal's
  news page.

## Formatting and submission

- Submit through Editorial Manager. Springer's standard manuscript preparation
  and reference-style guidelines apply; check the current submission-guidelines
  page for the required LaTeX/Word template.
- The journal accepts original research, reviews, discussion papers, and
  proposals for special issues; software and book reviews are handled separately.
- Include standard declarations (funding, competing interests, data
  availability, author contributions) as required by Springer Nature.

## Fit for the residual-plot paper

The manuscript *Automated Assessment of Residual Plots with Computer Vision
Models* sits squarely inside the stated scope: it applies computer vision and
machine learning to a core problem in statistical graphics (residual
diagnostics), and it contributes a methodological pipeline — image encoding, a
trained distance model, calibration, and a testing procedure — rather than only
an application. The explicit mentions of computer vision, machine learning,
graphics, and image processing make this a natural venue.

Points to weigh:

- Strong topical fit and a methods-oriented readership.
- Bi-monthly schedule and a relatively small annual volume.
- From 2027, publication requires an APC of about **$2,990 / €2,390**;
  submissions from 1 July 2026 already fall under the new rule.
- The 2026 editorial resignations introduce some uncertainty about review
  capacity during the transition.
